\subsection{The symplectic form on the audit double}\label{sec:symplectic-double}

Keep the notation of Lemma~\ref{lem:sigma-aud} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}: $\hat\Sigma=\Sigma_{1,1}\cup_\rho\Sigma_{1,1}$,
$\hat R=R_{\mathrm{aud}}\cup_{\sigma_{\mathrm{aud}}}R_{\mathrm{aud}}$,
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}=V_{\mathrm{aud}}\cup_{\sigma_{\mathrm{aud}}}V_{\mathrm{aud}}$,
with $\hat\pi\colon\hat R\to\hat\Sigma$ the projection and $F$, $\hat\Gamma$
the fiber and doubled section of that corollary.

\begin{lemma}[symplectic form on the audit double]\label{lem:double-form}
Let $\Omega$ be the positive area form on $F$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:bundle-symplectic-form}, preserved by $\varphi_0$ and
$\psi_0$.  Choose the positive area form $\eta$ on $\Sigma_{1,1}$ so that on
a collar $\partial\Sigma_{1,1}\times(-\varepsilon,0]$ with boundary parameter
$t\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ and outward-increasing collar coordinate $s$ it
equals $ds\wedge dt$, and so that the collar is disjoint from the annular
collars of $\alpha,\beta$ used in that lemma.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\eta$ on each copy glues to a smooth positive area form
 $\hat\eta$ on $\hat\Sigma$;
\item[(ii)] the vertical form $\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}$ on each copy glues to
 a smooth closed vertical form $\hat\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}$ on $\hat R$;
\item[(iii)] for every $K>0$,
 $\hat\omega_K=\hat\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}+K\hat\pi^*\hat\eta$ is a
 symplectic form on $\hat R$ restricting to $\omega_K$ on each copy;
\item[(iv)] $\hat\omega_K$ is positive on $F$ and on $\hat\Gamma$;
\item[(v)] the four surgery tori of Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}(b) are
 Lagrangian for $\hat\omega_K$, and each has a protected neighborhood with
 the split coordinates of Lemma~\ref{lem:bundle-symplectic-form}, disjoint
 from the seam collar $\hat\pi^{-1}(\partial\Sigma_{1,1}\times(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon))$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
(i) On the seam collar of $\hat\Sigma$ use the chart
$(t,s)\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\times(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ in which
$s\le0$ is the first copy's collar and, for $s\ge0$, the point $(t,s)$ is the
second copy's collar point with its own coordinates $t'=\rho(t)=1-t$ and
$s'=-s$: the second copy's outward direction is toward the seam, so its
outward-increasing coordinate is $-s$.  In its own coordinates the second
copy's form is $ds'\wedge dt'=d(-s)\wedge d(1-t)=ds\wedge dt$.  So the two
copies of $\eta$ are the single form $ds\wedge dt$ on the whole chart, and
$\hat\eta$ is smooth.  It is positive on both copies since each is oriented
as $\Sigma_{1,1}$ and $\hat\Sigma$ restricts to those orientations
(Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}(a)).

(ii) Over the seam collar the flat product atlas of
Lemma~\ref{lem:bundle-symplectic-form} presents the bundle as
$M_h\times(-\varepsilon,0]$ with $\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}$ equal to $\Omega$
on every fiber; this is well defined because the seam map $h$ of $M_h$ is a
product of the clutching maps and so preserves $\Omega$.  Extend the gluing map over the two collars as
$(z,t,s)\mapsto(\varphi_0 z,1-t,-s)$; this fixes the smooth structure of
$\hat R$ near the seam (any two collar choices give diffeomorphic doubles),
and in the flat atlas it is, on every fiber, the single map $\varphi_0$,
which is locally constant in the base and preserves $\Omega$.  Hence the transition maps of the flat atlas of $\hat R$
obtained by adjoining $\sigma_{\mathrm{aud}}$ all lie in the group generated
by $\varphi_0$ and $\psi_0$, all preserve $\Omega$, and the fiberwise form
$\Omega$ descends to a globally defined vertical form
$\hat\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}$ on $\hat R$.  It is closed because in each flat
product chart it is the pullback of the closed form $\Omega$ from the fiber
factor.

(iii) $d\hat\omega_K=0$ by (i) and (ii), and with the fiber and base
orientations fixed by $\Omega$ and $\hat\eta$,
$\hat\omega_K^2=2K\,\hat\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}\wedge\hat\pi^*\hat\eta>0$,
exactly as in Lemma~\ref{lem:bundle-symplectic-form}.  On each copy the form
is $\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}+K\pi^*\eta=\omega_K$ by construction.

(iv) $F$ is a fiber, on which $\hat\omega_K$ restricts to $\Omega>0$.
$\hat\Gamma$ is the doubled section through the common fixed point $p$; in
every flat product chart it is $\{p\}\times U$, so
$\hat\Omega_{\mathrm{vert}}$ restricts to zero on it and
$\hat\omega_K|_{\hat\Gamma}=K\hat\eta>0$.

(v) Each of the four tori lies in the interior of one copy, over the
annular collar of $\alpha$ or $\beta$ in that copy, which is disjoint from
the seam collar by the choice of $\eta$.  On that copy $\hat\omega_K=\omega_K$,
so Lemma~\ref{lem:bundle-symplectic-form} applies verbatim: the torus has
one fiber tangent and one base tangent, both summands vanish on it, and the
protected split coordinates are the ones displayed there.  The mirror tori
are the images of $T_\alpha,T_\beta$ under the swap of the two copies, which
is an isomorphism of the second copy with $R_{\mathrm{aud}}$ carrying
$\hat\omega_K|_{\text{copy }2}$ to $\omega_K$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is symplectic]\label{cor:Z-symplectic}
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ carries a symplectic form $\hat\omega$ that agrees with
$\hat\omega_K$ outside the four surgery neighborhoods, restricts positively
to $F$ and to $\hat\Gamma$, and is obtained from $(\hat R,\hat\omega_K)$ by
four Luttinger surgeries in the sense of \cite[Definition~2.1]{ADK}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:double-form}(v) and Theorem~\ref{thm:framing} applied in
each copy, the four tori are Lagrangian and the four unit-coefficient filling
slopes are their Lagrangian-framing classes; the surgeries of
Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}(b) are therefore Luttinger surgeries, and
\cite[Proposition~2.2]{ADK} supplies, one surgery at a time, a symplectic
form agreeing with the previous one outside the surgered neighborhood.  The
four neighborhoods are disjoint from one another, from $F$, and from
$\hat\Gamma$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}(b)), so the final form still
restricts positively to $F$ and $\hat\Gamma$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:double-form}(iv).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hypotheses of the external results, discharged}
\label{sec:external-hypotheses}

The existence theorem below uses five results from the literature.  Each is
stated with its hypotheses, and each hypothesis is discharged by a statement
about the audit object proved in this article.  No comparison with any
other author's construction enters.

\begin{description}
\item[Van Kampen.] $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}=V_{\mathrm{aud}}\cup_{\partial}V_{\mathrm{aud}}$
 with connected, bicollared common boundary $\partial V_{\mathrm{aud}}=M_h$
 and $\pione(V_{\mathrm{aud}})=1$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:audit-manifold});
 hence $\pione(Z_{\mathrm{aud}})=1$.
\item[Freedman \cite{Freedman}.] Closed simply connected topological
 4-manifolds are classified by intersection form and Kirby--Siebenmann
 invariant, the latter determined by $\sigma/8$ when the form is even.
 Hypotheses discharged in Proposition~\ref{prop:Z-topology}: simple
 connectivity, $b_2=2$, form $H$ on the basis $(F,\hat\Gamma)$,
 smoothness (so Kirby--Siebenmann vanishes).
\item[Thurston \cite{Thurston}.] A closed surface bundle over a surface with
 homologically essential fiber is symplectic with symplectic fibers.  We do
 not invoke it: Lemma~\ref{lem:double-form} constructs the form directly
 (the fiber is essential anyway, since $F\cdot\hat\Gamma=1$).
\item[Auroux--Donaldson--Katzarkov \cite{ADK} (Luttinger \cite{Luttinger}).]
 Luttinger surgery on a Lagrangian torus along its Lagrangian framing yields
 a symplectic manifold with a form agreeing with the old one away from the
 torus.  Hypotheses discharged in Lemma~\ref{lem:double-form}(v) and
 Theorem~\ref{thm:framing}: Lagrangian tori, Lagrangian-framed slopes.
\item[Ho--Li \cite{HoLi}.] Luttinger surgery preserves the symplectic
 Kodaira dimension.  Hypothesis: the surgery is Luttinger
 (Corollary~\ref{cor:Z-symplectic}).
\item[Li \cite{Li}.] The symplectic Kodaira dimension of a closed
 symplectic 4-manifold is a diffeomorphism invariant, equal to $-\infty$
 exactly for rational and ruled manifolds.  Hypothesis: a closed symplectic
 4-manifold (Corollary~\ref{cor:Z-symplectic}).
\end{description}

\begin{proposition}[topology of $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$]\label{prop:Z-topology}
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is closed, simply connected, and spin, with
$b_2(Z_{\mathrm{aud}})=2$, signature $0$, and intersection form $H$ on the
basis $(F,\hat\Gamma)$.  Consequently $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is homeomorphic to
$S^2\times S^2$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Closed: $\partial V_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is glued to itself.  Simply connected
by van Kampen as above.  $\chi(Z_{\mathrm{aud}})=2\chi(V_{\mathrm{aud}})-\chi(M_h)=4-0=4$
(the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:audit-manifold} gives
$\chi(V_{\mathrm{aud}})=2$; a mapping torus has Euler characteristic $0$),
so $b_2=2$ with $H_2$ torsion-free.  By
Corollary~\ref{cor:audit-double}(c), $F\cdot F=0$, $\hat\Gamma\cdot\hat\Gamma=0$,
$F\cdot\hat\Gamma=1$: the Gram matrix of $(F,\hat\Gamma)$ is $H$, of
determinant $-1$, hence unimodular; a unimodular rank-two sublattice of the
unimodular rank-two lattice $H_2(Z_{\mathrm{aud}})$ has index one, so
$(F,\hat\Gamma)$ is a basis and the form is $H$.  $H$ is even, so
$w_2=0$ by Wu's formula and $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is spin; its signature is
$0$.  $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is smooth, so its Kirby--Siebenmann invariant
vanishes, and Freedman's classification gives
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}\cong S^2\times S^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[an exotic $S^2\times S^2$]\label{thm:exotic}
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is a closed symplectic 4-manifold that is homeomorphic
but not diffeomorphic to $S^2\times S^2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Symplectic and homeomorphic to $S^2\times S^2$ by
Corollary~\ref{cor:Z-symplectic} and Proposition~\ref{prop:Z-topology}.
For non-diffeomorphism: $\hat R$ is a surface bundle with aspherical fiber
and base, hence aspherical, so it contains no essential sphere; a
symplectic 4-manifold with $\pi_2=0$ is minimal (an exceptional sphere
would be null-homotopic, hence of square $0$, not $-1$) and is neither
rational nor ruled (those have $\pi_2\ne0$).  By \cite{Li},
$\kappa(\hat R,\hat\omega_K)\ne-\infty$.  By \cite{HoLi}, each of the four
Luttinger surgeries preserves $\kappa$, so $\kappa(Z_{\mathrm{aud}},\hat\omega)\ne-\infty$.
Since $S^2\times S^2$ is rational, every symplectic form on it has
$\kappa=-\infty$, and by \cite{Li} $\kappa$ depends only on the
diffeomorphism type.  Hence $Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is not diffeomorphic to
$S^2\times S^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[what the theorem does and does not say]
Theorem~\ref{thm:exotic} is a statement about the object of
Definition~\ref{def:Vaudit} and its double.  It does not assert that
$Z_{\mathrm{aud}}$ is the manifold constructed by Wuebben \cite{Wuebben} or
by Lidman--Piccirillo \cite{LidmanPiccirillo}; that comparison is the
subject of Section~\ref{sec:relation} and its checklist, and the contrary
fundamental-group computation reported there stands unreconciled and may
or may not concern this member.  Nor does the theorem depend on any
statement in that section.
\end{remark}
