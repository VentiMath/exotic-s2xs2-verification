# The Lidman--Piccirillo disagreement: what is public and what is proved here

## Incident summary

Wuebben's public repository has disclosed from its first visible commit
(`7ee4f196`, 2026-08-06) that Lidman and Piccirillo reported a computation
with `pi_1(V) != 1` and that the disagreement was unresolved.  The current
README at `82fbf7ef` repeats that statement.  This was therefore not an
August 28 discovery or a new objection to release `v1.5.6`.

This project also knew the fact; it was recorded in the working notes on
2026-08-23.  Subsequent work answered the
specific based-versus-unbased monodromy objection for this project's marked
model, but the unresolved contrary computation was not promoted into the
proof ledger, `README.md`, `STATUS.md`, or the verification manuscript.  The
later phrase "no known project-specific gap" therefore overstated the state
of knowledge.  It meant that no internal certificate boundary remained in
the chosen model; it did not mean that the competing geometric computation
had been reconciled.

## What can be recovered without private correspondence

The public Lidman--Piccirillo v1 source does not contain their contrary
fundamental-group presentation.  It proves only that `pi_1(V)` is normally
generated by the fiber group.  It also explicitly says that the section
curves parametrizing the two surgery tori were chosen somewhat arbitrarily
and that `V` is not well-defined until those choices are fixed.  Consequently
the public source admits two possibilities that cannot be distinguished from
the available files:

1. their reported computation uses a different pair of section curves and
   hence a genuinely different member of the allowed family; or
2. it intends Wuebben's specified member but differs in a based transport,
   peripheral word, framing, or additional complement relation.

Wuebben's public history contains no copy of their relation sheet, generator
dictionary, or nontriviality witness.  There are no public issues or pull
requests supplying one.  The exact first divergent relation therefore cannot
be reconstructed from the public record alone.

## The load-bearing relation R3

Wuebben's finite presentations become tractable only after adjoining

`B (s^-1 r^-1 y x) B^-1 = r^-1 s^-1 x`.

The old Run 23 checker established the word and based monodromy identity in
an unpunctured beta mapping cylinder.  The full complement computation then
compared only a low-index fingerprint with Wuebben's drilled relation group.
That fingerprint was explicitly non-discriminating.  Thus the prior label
"R3 certified geometrically" omitted one necessary check: the transport
homotopy had to live in the complement of both surgery tori.

Run 67 supplies that check.  It first detects the subtlety rather than hiding
it: the literal `kappa_3` path is disjoint from `c` but crosses `e` once, so
the beta *core* stack meets `T_beta` (264 common vertices).  The paper uses a
parallel beta loop.  The complete 17,664-tetrahedron mapping-cylinder stack
at that parallel level embeds in the full triangulated bundle with zero
vertices on either `T_alpha` or `T_beta`.  A second `2 x 35` simplicial grid
at the fixed basepoint `p` homotopes the parallel beta loop to the whiskered
core loop used for the presentation, again with zero torus hits.  Combined
with Run 23's based monodromy certificate, this proves R3 in this project's
actual triangulated torus complement.

As an algebraic cross-check, direct Knuth--Bendix reduction of the R3 residual
in the sealed `3`-generator, `78`-relator complement is inconclusive because
the system is not confluent.  The residual nevertheless maps to the identity
in the class-6 `2`-, `3`-, and `5`-group quotients.  These quotient results
are supporting evidence only; the complement-level stack embedding is the
positive geometric certificate.

## Present conclusion

The newly completed check makes R3 substantially less likely to be the
source of the disagreement *within the marked triangulated model*.  It does
not identify what Lidman and Piccirillo computed, because their contrary
presentation is not public.  The correct publication posture is therefore:

- the sealed presentations and their triviality certificates are valid;
- the construction-specific bridge has extensive independent checks,
  including the new complement-level R3 certificate;
- no discrepancy has been found within those public, reproducible checks;
- a publicly acknowledged contrary computation remains unreconciled and
  must be disclosed; and
- the claim must not be described as having "no known project-specific gap"
  until the competing presentation can be compared relation by relation or
  an equivalent independent calculation settles the same specified
  parametrization.
