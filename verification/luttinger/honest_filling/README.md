# The honest filling on the sheet loops

Working directory beside the sealed chain; nothing here is part of the sealed chain. Large kbprog outputs (`*.kbprog`, `*.reduce`, `*.ec`, `*.proof.log`) are kept out of the repository by `.gitignore`; inputs, scripts, logs and documents are tracked.

## The finding

The sealed beta filling relator is `geom_N . lb_b_s2^{sign_b}` with
`geom_N = A . N_grid^-1 . A^-1` (`r_run.py`): the grid meridian whiskered
along `A` and then `s_2`, beside a longitude whiskered along `s_2` alone.
A Dehn filling relator needs one whisker for both.  The relator with the
common `s_2` whisker is `N_grid^-1 . lb_b_s2^{sign_b}`, and as free words
`N_grid^-1 = A^-1 . geom_N . A`.  The two relators generate the same normal
closure only if `[A, N_grid]` dies in the filled group, which nothing
certifies (the complement system leaves a 44-letter residual).  So the eight
honest filled groups are `Q / << alpha relator, A^-1 N A . lambda_beta^{sign_b} >>`,
and each is the normal closure of the single commutator `[A, N_grid]`
modulo the sealed certificates.

The boundary-tori computation in `simplicial_filling/` reaches the same
relators: its alpha words are cyclic conjugates of the sealed alpha
relators, and its beta words are conjugate to the common-whisker product
(abelian boundary three-torus; confirmed formally in every halted system).

## What is certified here

`honest_filling.json` fixes, on the sheet loops `x,y,r,s,A,B,M,N`:

* 30 relations that hold in `Q`: the eight relation-sheet identities that do
  not involve the `x` transport, and the 19 relators of GAP's modified
  Todd-Coxeter presentation of `Q` on the sheet loops.  Every one reduces to
  the empty word under the complement-only rewriting system
  (`certify_relations.py`), which is a proof in `pi_1(C_aud)`.
* four identities tying the filling words to sheet words, likewise reduced:
  `lb_a_y1 = A x`, `lb_b_s2 = r^-1 M r B`, `geom_M_y2 = y^-1 M y`,
  `lb_a_y2 = y^-1 A r^-1 y`.
* the eight honest filling pairs as sheet words and as `Q` words.

The sheet loops generate `Q` (sealed, `generation_check/`), so the group on
the sheet loops with the 26 relations maps onto `Q`, and adding a filling
pair maps it onto the honest filled group.  Triviality of any of these eight
small presentations therefore proves the corresponding honest group trivial.

## What is not yet certified

The relation sheet prints `B x B^-1 = y^-1`.  For the audit loops the
certified transport is `B x B^-1 = y^-1 M`, and the drilled-fiber row
likewise gains `M`; the printed forms would follow only if `M = 1` in `Q`,
which is open (`M` is trivial in every finite quotient of `Q` tested).  With
the printed `y^-1` row every honest case collapses by coset enumeration in
milliseconds, but that row is not a certified relation, so the collapse
proves nothing.

With the 30 certified relations (`collapse_full.g`) coset enumeration
proves the sealed fillings trivial in seven of the eight cases, in
agreement with the sealed KBMAG certificates, and overflows on all eight
honest cases.  No finite quotient of `Q` up to PSL(2,17) kills an honest
filling pair, so nothing refutes triviality either.  The honest groups are
undecided.

Tests that would have proved a group nontrivial, all negative on all eight cases:

- no epimorphism onto any nonabelian simple group of order below 546 312
  (GQuotients over SimpleGroupsIterator; a perfect group with a nontrivial finite
  quotient has a nonabelian simple quotient, so this covers every finite quotient of
  that size);
- adding one power relator a^n, b^n or (ab)^n (n = 2..7) gives either the trivial group
  or an overflow, never a nontrivial finite quotient;
- numerical Levenberg-Marquardt search for representations into SL(2,C) (120 random
  starts) and SL(3,C) (60 starts): every converged start is the trivial or central

None of this is evidence either way; the honest groups remain undecided.

### Two single-commutator characterizations (2026-09-02, later)

- Sealed vs honest beta relator: each honest group is the normal closure of `[A, N_grid]` (the alpha
  base loop against the s2-whiskered beta meridian); the sealed certificates prove the quotient by it
  trivial.
- Wuebben's Table 1 (arXiv:2608.17267v1) transported into the audit letters by the dictionary
  `y -> M^-1 y`, `s -> r^-1 M^-1 r s`, `M -> B M B^-1`, `N -> M^-1 N M` (nine non-filling rows
  certified as identities of Q by reduction in the complement system): coset enumeration on the 30
  certified relations plus the honest fillings plus the single relator `[B, M]` gives index 1 in all
  eight cases (3 000 000 cosets), while every case overflows without it.  So each honest group is also
  the normal closure of `[B, M]` (the beta base loop against the y1-whiskered alpha meridian).

Hence pi_1(V_aud) = 1 iff [B, M] = 1 in the honest group iff [A, N_grid] = 1 there.  Neither
commutator is decided.

## Direct double: the boundary-word arrangement is load-bearing (2026-09-03)

The direct-double presentation (`verification/luttinger/direct_z/` in the
other working tree, v2.5.0 release candidate) glues two copies of the honest
filled group along the fiber seams `x_L = r_R, y_L = s_R, r_L = x_R, s_L = y_R`
(phi_0 on the based fiber generators, transport taken to be trivial) and the
boundary relation `delta_L delta_R = 1` with `delta = A^-1 B^-1 A B`, which
GEOMETRIC_BRIDGE.md calls a chosen orientation.  Two tests of that choice live
in `direct_z_variants/`.  All inputs are in Q letters (3 generators per copy,
12 kbmag letters), built from `sealed_transport/r_presentations.json` exactly as
`build_direct_z_q.py` does; only the seams and the boundary word vary.

|---|---|---|---|
| `ctrl_fiber_seams_only` | phi_0 | none | no collapse (99,976 eqns) |
| `ctrl_delta_only_no_fiber_seams` | none | `d_L d_R`, d = A^-1B^-1AB | no collapse |
| `ctrl_wrong_fiber_seams_plus_delta` | identity (`g_L = g_R`) | `d_L d_R`, d = A^-1B^-1AB | **collapses to 1** |
| `prod_AiBiAB` (= the certified presentation) | phi_0 | `d_L d_R`, d = A^-1B^-1AB | collapses to 1 |
| `prod_BAiBiA`, `prod_BiAiBA`, `prod_AiBABi` | phi_0 | `d_L d_R` | collapse to 1 |
| `prod_BiABAi`, `prod_ABAiBi`, `prod_BABiAi`, `prod_ABiAiB` | phi_0 | `d_L d_R` | **no collapse** in 100k eqns |
| `eqn_*` (`d_L = d_R`, wrong orientation for sigma_aud) | phi_0 | | AiBiAB, BiABAi, BAiBiA, BiAiBA, AiBABi, ABiAiB collapse; ABAiBi, BABiAi do not |

Two facts follow.  The identity-seam control collapses, so a collapse does
not test the seam content at all.  And the collapse depends on which cyclic
conjugate of `[A,B]^{+-1}` is used: four of the eight arrangements collapse,
four do not.  The arrangement is therefore load-bearing and has to be derived,
not chosen.

### 2. Seam-consistency identity (`seam_consistency.py`, `seam_residuals.py`)

In the boundary mapping torus `M_h`, with `t` the p-section circle,
`t g t^-1 = h^{-+1}(g)` for every fiber loop `g`, where `h` is the boundary
monodromy of the *unsurgered* bundle (the tori are interior).  Pushing this
into `pi_1(V_aud, q)` along a base arc `gamma` from the boundary to `q` gives

    delta_gamma . tau_gamma(g) . delta_gamma^-1 = tau_gamma(h^{-+1}(g)),

where `delta_gamma` is the cyclic conjugate of `[A,B]^{+-1}` fixed by the
corner of the spine vertex that `gamma` leaves through, and `tau_gamma` is the
transport of the fiber loops along `gamma` (trivial iff the membrane
`g x gamma` misses both tori).  `h` is computed from the unsurgered monodromy
actions, the same ones `sigma_aud/sigma_aud_check.py` uses:
`Phi: x<->r, y<->s`; `Psi: x->y^-1, y->yx, r->r, s->s`.  A pair
`(delta, tau)` that appears in a doubled presentation must satisfy this
identity in the honest filled group; it is a necessary condition, certified by
reduction to the empty word in the halted 500k shortlex system for
`honest_y1_p1_p1` (non-reduction is inconclusive).

Result with `tau = id`, all eight arrangements (`seam_consistency.log`):

| delta | residual lengths (x, y, r, s) | consistent with trivial transport |
|---|---|---|
| A^-1B^-1AB (certified) | 0, 0, 2, 7 | not certified |
| B^-1ABA^-1 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | **yes** |
| ABA^-1B^-1 | 2, 7, 4, 8 | not certified |
| BA^-1B^-1A | 3, 8, 1, 8 | not certified |
| B^-1A^-1BA | 0, 0, 1, 9 | not certified |
| A^-1BAB^-1 | 2, 8, 3, 8 | not certified |
| BAB^-1A^-1 | 3, 6, 1, 6 | not certified |
| AB^-1A^-1B | 0, 0, 0, 0 | **yes** |

The two consistent arrangements are inverse to each other, as they must be
(the identity is invariant under `delta -> delta^-1`, `h -> h^-1`).  For the
certified `delta = A^-1B^-1AB` the residuals in sheet letters are
(`seam_residuals.log`)

    g = r:  delta r delta^-1 = (r s) . [M B]
    g = s:  delta s delta^-1 = (r^-1) . [A^-1 B^-1 M^-1 N M B A]

and the `g = r` residual is certified equal to `r A^-1 N^-1 A r^-1`, which is
what one gets by hand: `A^-1B^-1AB = A^-1 (B^-1ABA^-1) A`, so conjugating the
consistent arrangement by `A` costs exactly one uncorrected `A s A^-1 = N y`
row.  Hence `(id, A^-1B^-1AB)` is the seam data of the double based at q only if
`M B = 1` in the honest filled group; `M B = 1` implies `[B,M] = 1`, and
`[B,M] = 1` alone collapses `honest_y1_p1_p1` to the trivial group
(single-relator table above).  So unless `pi_1(V_aud) = 1` already, the
certified presentation does not carry the seam data of `Z_aud` at q.

What the certified presentation does prove (`wrong_vs_derived.g`, checked in
the free group on the four base letters): with `delta_0 = A B^-1 A^-1 B` the
derived word and `delta_1 = A^-1 B^-1 A B` the certified one,
`delta_1 = A^-1 delta_0^-1 A`, and modulo the derived relation
`delta_0_L delta_0_R = 1`

    delta_1_L delta_1_R  =  A_L^-1 [delta_0_L^-1, A_L A_R^-1] A_L .

So the certified group `W` is the derived double `D` with the one extra
relation `[delta_0_L, A_L A_R^-1] = 1`, and `W = 1` says exactly that

    D  =  normal closure of the single commutator  [delta_0_L, A_L A_R^-1] ,

the analogue for the double of `H_aud = <<[B,M]>>`.  It does not say `D = 1`.
Three cases are open, not two: `pi_1(V_aud) = 1` (then everything is
trivial); `pi_1(V_aud) != 1` but `D = 1` (the double simply connected for a
real reason; then W happens to present it, though not with the geometric seam
data); or `D != 1`.  The certificate does not distinguish them.

What would settle #819: derive `gamma`, `delta_gamma` and `tau_gamma` in the
audit complex for one corner (the other three corners then follow by
conjugating with `A^{+-1}`, `B^{+-1}` and correcting with the rows), check the
derived pair against this identity, and run kbprog on that presentation.

### 3. The boundary class from the base 2-complex (`direct_z_variants/base_boundary/`)

The audit base (bundle.py docstring, r_run.py `geometric_paper_candidates`) is
an annulus (angular copies A0, A1, A2 with the phi_0 edge A2-A0; radii J0, J1,
J2) plus the beta band (stack levels x thickness tau0, tau1, tau2) whose feet
are glued to the arcs A0-A1-A2 at J2 and at J0.  Its free boundary is the
four-arc loop (phi_0 edge at J2) + (band side tau2) + (phi_0 edge at J0) +
(band side tau0), and the base point q = (A0, p, J2) lies on it, so there is
no transport arc at all.  `alpha_positive` is the outer circle at J2,
`beta_positive` goes A0 -> A1 at J2, down the band core at tau1, back out
radially at A1, and A1 -> A0; `A` and `B` are their inverses.
`base_boundary_word.g` builds this 2-complex (chi = -1, boundary = the six
free edges), computes its edge-path group (free of rank 2, generated by
alpha_positive and beta_positive) and expresses the boundary loop based at q:

    boundary loop at q  =  A B^-1 A^-1 B   (inverse: B^-1 A B A^-1)

This is exactly the pair singled out by the seam-consistency identity in
section 2, obtained independently of it.  The certified boundary relation uses
`A^-1 B^-1 A B`, which is `A^-1 (B^-1 A B A^-1) A`: the conjugate of the true
boundary class by A, i.e. the boundary loop based at q after one extra turn
around alpha, without the corresponding transport of the fiber seams.  In the
surgered group that conjugation is not free (`A s A^-1 = N y`), which is the
`M B` residual of section 2.

### 5. The boundary class in the section of the real complex (`direct_z_variants/section_complex/`)

`extract_section.py` builds the real bundle K (`build_bundle(dir_b=1,
dir_a=-1)`, as in `r_run.main`), extracts the subcomplex of all simplices
whose vertices carry the fiber label p (the p-section), and records the three
based edge paths used by r_run.py (`alpha_positive`, `beta_positive`) and the
boundary path through the free edges.  The section has V = 102, E = 243,
F = 140, chi = -1, 66 free edges, and the boundary path uses exactly those 66
edges.  `section_boundary_word.g` computes its edge-path group (free of rank 2,
generated by `alpha_positive` and `beta_positive`) and the class of the
boundary loop at q (`section_boundary_word.log`):

    boundary loop at q  =  A B^-1 A^-1 B   (inverse B^-1 A B A^-1)

in the section of the real complex, with the very edge paths whose loop words
are `geom_A` and `geom_B`.  The p-section misses both tori (p lies on neither
c nor e), so this identity holds in the complement group and hence in Q.
Three derivations agree: the hand model (section 3), the boundary-monodromy
identity (section 2), and the real section.  What remains for the pipeline
is to carry the boundary path through the Tietze certificate as a tracked
word, so the doubled presentation reads it from the sealed transport.

### 6. Fiber seams as literal paths; the gate (`section_complex/phi0_fiber_seams.py`, `seam_gate.py`)

`phi0_fiber_seams.py`: phi_0 is an involution on all 86 fiber vertices, fixes
p, and carries each of the four based rail loops onto its partner vertex by
vertex: phi_0(x) = r, phi_0(y) = s, phi_0(r) = x, phi_0(s) = y (length-10
edge paths).  So the four fiber seams of the double are certified identities
of based paths, not notation.

`seam_gate.py` (issue #823) is the check any doubled presentation must pass
before kbprog: G1 the seam map is an involution of the free fiber group
conjugating the unsurgered boundary monodromy `h_delta` to its inverse
(free-group computation); G2 the pairing identity
`delta tau(g) delta^-1 = tau(h_delta g)` reduces to 1 in the filled group;
G3 the boundary relation is `delta_L delta_R = 1`.  Results in
`seam_gate.log` / `seam_gate.json`: the derived presentation passes; the
v2.5.0 presentation fails G2; the identity-seam control fails G1; the six
other arrangements fail G2; the `delta_L = delta_R` form fails G3.
