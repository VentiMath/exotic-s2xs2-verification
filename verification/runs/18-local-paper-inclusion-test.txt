Run 18: paper-relator inclusion in historical local quotients (2026-08-23)
============================================================================

For each of the eight historical arbitrary-corner/tree-based filling
presentations, KBMAG constructed its available nonconfluent rewriting system.
All eight coherent paper filling-relator pairs (two half-drifts times four
signs) were then reduced inside that local quotient.

No local quotient reduced both relators of any paper pair to the identity.
All eight outcomes are INCONCLUSIVE because the local rewriting systems are
nonconfluent. This is not evidence that a local group is nontrivial and does
not weaken the complete confluent certificates for the paper-coordinate
groups. It shows only that normal-form inclusion is not a shortcut to the
missing simultaneous peripheral conjugacy calculation.

Reproduce with:

  python3 group_attack.py local-paper-inclusion-all
