Local N identification (2026-08-23)
===================================

Target
------

Identify the 12-edge detour in the punctured M1 alpha-transport grid with the
specifically s_2-based meridian N of T_beta, including orientation.

Construction
------------

The s-path meets e at the fiber vertex

  s_e = ('d', -1, 4).

Let u=('d',-1,3) be its predecessor.  In the punctured alpha grid, the deleted
edge u--s_e is replaced by the five-edge path

  A0 u -> A1 u -> A2 s_e -> A2 ('d',-1,5)
       -> A1 ('d',-1,5) -> A0 s_e.

Closing this detour by the reversed original edge s_e--u gives a loop based at
the endpoint of the literal paper whisker s_2.  This is the grid-defined N.

Local certificate
-----------------

Take the union of the vertices of all 4-simplices containing the crossing,
remove the torus vertices, and retain the grid and dual-meridian paths.  Its
induced complement has f-vector

  [24, 90, 108, 42].

Its spanning-tree presentation reduces by elementary Tietze elimination to
one generator and no relators, hence is the infinite cyclic local meridian
group.  Four oriented dual meridians based at s_e are reachable in this local
complex.  For every one of them:

  grid_meridian^-1 * oriented_dual_meridian  ->  1,
  grid_meridian^-1 * oriented_dual_meridian^-1 -> g^2 or g^-2.

Every elimination is replayed by `verify_certificate`.  Thus the traced grid
detour is exactly the positively oriented dual meridian N, and is not N^-1.
The result is local and does not depend on solving the difficult global
complement word problem.

Implementation
--------------

`alpha_s_transport_sweep` in `luttinger/r_run.py` performs the construction,
asserts the rank-one result, replays the Tietze certificate, and exports the
based word as `alpha_s_grid_N`.  The Table 1 comparison now uses this word as
`geom_N`.
