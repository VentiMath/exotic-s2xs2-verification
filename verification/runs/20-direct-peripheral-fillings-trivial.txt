Historical-coordinate map
-------------------------

  old drift 1 (down) = inverse(n1_y2 section),
  old drift 2 (up)   = inverse(n0_y1 section),

whisker to a connected boundary component conjugates every peripheral element

  drift 1 -> n1_y2,  (eA,eB) -> (-eA,-eB),
