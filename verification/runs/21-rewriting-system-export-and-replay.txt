Run 21: hashed rewriting-system export and replay (2026-08-23)
================================================================

Purpose
-------
Turn the all-eight GAP/KBMAG console result from Run 20 into durable,
machine-readable artifacts which can be integrity-checked and replayed.

Export
------
`group_attack.py direct-paper-export` reconstructs the eight directly traced
peripheral fillings from `r_presentations.json`. It uses fill-then-base
relator order in seven cases and the successful base-then-fill order for the
identical hard relator set n0_y1 (+1,+1). It refuses success unless KBMAG
reports a confluent system and every simplified generator has identity normal
form.

For each case, `luttinger/direct_rws/` contains:

  * `.presentation`: the simplified free generators and relators;
  * `.certificate`: the Knuth--Bendix return value, confluence flag, generator
    normal forms, and certified-trivial flag;
  * `.rws`: the completed KBMAG rewriting system.

`manifest.json` records the half-drift, signs, relator ordering, SHA-256 of
`r_presentations.json`, SHA-256 of the exact generated GAP export program, and
SHA-256 of all 24 case artifacts. The completed systems are exceptionally
small: their rules send the generators (and inverse letters) to the identity.

Replay
------
`group_attack.py direct-paper-replay` verifies all 24 artifact digests before
starting GAP. It loads each `.rws` through KBMAG's `ReadRWS`, resets the saved
state, rebuilds the reduction engine from the stored equations, checks
confluence, and reduces every free-structure generator.

Exact replay result:

  verified input and export-program digests plus 24 artifact digests
  n0_y1 (+1,+1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n0_y1 (+1,-1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n0_y1 (-1,+1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n0_y1 (-1,-1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n1_y2 (+1,+1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n1_y2 (+1,-1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n1_y2 (-1,+1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  n1_y2 (-1,-1): four free-structure letters -> identity, ok=true
  ALL EIGHT EXPORTED SYSTEMS REPLAYED

The four free-structure letters are the two simplified group generators and
their formal inverses; each `.certificate` records the corresponding two group
generator normal forms.

Reproduce from `luttinger/` with GAP + KBMAG available:

  python3 group_attack.py direct-paper-export \
    --gap-file r_direct_paper_export.g
  python3 group_attack.py direct-paper-replay \
    --gap-file r_direct_paper_replay.g

Trust boundary
--------------
This certifies the finite-presentation calculation conditional on the exported
triangulation-derived relators and on GAP/KBMAG's Tietze simplification and
completion implementations. It does not independently certify the geometric
derivation of those relators or the paper's symplectic framing lemma; those
remain separate layers of the audit.
