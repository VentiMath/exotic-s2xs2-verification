Scope
-----

fundamental-group computation and found no gap.  It was a hypothesis audit:
it checked that the cited theorems' hypotheses match the constructed
manifolds and executed the finite algebra.  This run replaces it with a
proof-grade dependency chain.  Every implication from the certified
statement pi_1(V) = 1 (Theorem D) to the paper's Theorems A, B and C is an
explicit item of one of four kinds:

Both checkers verify that every citation resolves, that the dependency

Differences from the paper's route worth recording
--------------------------------------------------

* H_1(V) = 0 is taken from the certificate (pi_1(V) = 1), not from the
  paper's Lemma 4.2 and the surgery relations; Lemma 4.2 is needed only
  for the "forcing" direction of Proposition 1.3, which no theorem uses.
* The intersection form of Z is identified as H from the certified
  Gamma_hat.Gamma_hat = 0 (run 28) together with F.F = 0 and F.Gamma_hat
  = 1, so that Z is spin because its form is even; the paper's route
  (Z spin as a double cover of the spin W, then Gamma_hat^2 even) is not
  needed for Theorem A.  W spin still enters, through Lidman--Piccirillo's
  Lemma 7, for the Hambleton--Kreck invariants and for Klug's theorem.
* The non-diffeomorphism Z != S^2 x S^2 records the orientation step
  explicitly: the independence of kappa from the symplectic form is over
  forms inducing a fixed orientation, so a hypothetical diffeomorphism is
  first made orientation-preserving with a reflection of one S^2 factor.
* The sliceness dichotomy is taken over Z/2 coefficients throughout, which
  is what makes the null-homologous case exactly Klug's characteristic
  case in the spin manifold W - B^4, and what makes the lifted torus in Z
  nonzero integrally (H_2(Z; Z/2) = H_2(Z) (x) Z/2 since H_1(Z) = 0).
* For Z'' the diagonalization of the odd form is an explicit change of
  basis valid for every odd Gamma''.Gamma'' = 2n+1, not an appeal to the
  classification of indefinite odd forms.
