Run 38: complement-presentation theorem boundary (2026-08-26)
================================================================

Purpose
-------

Decompose the former `T_complement_presentation` trust boundary and prove the
two elementary parts directly: the complement deformation retraction and the
maximal-tree presentation of its fundamental group.

Direct complement retraction
----------------------------

For a full subcomplex T, every simplex meets T in a face. At a point outside
T, the sum S of barycentric coordinates on vertices outside T is positive.
Set the T-coordinates to zero, divide the remaining coordinates by S, and
join the point to this image by a straight-line homotopy. The outside weight
along the homotopy is `(1-t)S+t`, hence never zero. The formulas agree on
faces and give a strong deformation retraction onto the induced subcomplex
on vertices outside T.

The checker exhausts all 52 nontrivial T/outside vertex partitions of
simplices in dimensions at most four. No regular-neighborhood or local-
flatness theorem is used for this homotopy equivalence.
