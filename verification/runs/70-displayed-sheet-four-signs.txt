Run 70: coset enumeration of the displayed relation sheet, four sign sheets (2026-09-02)
=========================================================================================

Question
--------

The paper's "Relation sheet and product fillings" subsection displays ten
relations (the surface relation, eight transport relations, the drilled-fiber
relation) and two longitudes, and says the four algebraic sign sheets append
`M lambda_alpha^{eps_A} = 1`, `N lambda_beta^{eps_B} = 1`.  An outside reader
(2026-09-01) reported encoding exactly those displayed words in GAP and getting
the trivial group for all four sheets in under a second, without touching the
sealed 78-relator presentation Q or any certificate.  Is that reproducible
from the displayed text alone, as our own artifact?

Frozen input
------------

`luttinger/displayed_sheet/displayed_sheet.g` transcribes the displayed words
literally.  Nothing is read from the repository: no JSON, no sealed
presentation, no certificate.  The commutator convention is the paper's own,
`[a,b] = a b a^-1 b^-1`, matching the surface word written out on the audit
octagon as `x y x^-1 y^-1 r s r^-1 s^-1`.  The enumeration is bounded
(`CosetTableDefaultMaxLimit := 2000000`) so a hard case fails rather than
hangs.

Result
------

Every filled sheet collapses to the trivial group.

    full sheet (+,+)  relators=12  |G|=1  H1=[ ]   ~3.2 s
    full sheet (+,-)  relators=12  |G|=1  H1=[ ]   ~3.7 s
    full sheet (-,+)  relators=12  |G|=1  H1=[ ]   ~0.17 s
    full sheet (-,-)  relators=12  |G|=1  H1=[ ]   ~0.18 s

The same four sheets with the drilled-fiber relation removed (eleven relators)
also collapse, roughly four to twelve times more slowly:

    no-drilled (+,+)  |G|=1   ~12.1 s
    no-drilled (+,-)  |G|=1   ~13.4 s
    no-drilled (-,+)  |G|=1   ~1.8 s
    no-drilled (-,-)  |G|=1   ~1.0 s

Negative controls: with only one filling relation adjoined, the abelianization
is infinite cyclic in both cases (`H1 = [ 0 ]`), so the enumeration is not
collapsing everything it is handed.  This matches the slope-family
abelianization proposition for the sealed presentation.

What this does and does not establish
-------------------------------------

Established: the ten displayed relations plus the two fillings force
triviality, for all four sign sheets, by coset enumeration alone.  The
78-relator presentation and the certificate replay are not needed to reach
this verdict.  The verdict is now a repository artifact with a recorded
environment, not a reviewer's report.

Also observed, feeding the separate redundancy question: triviality does not
depend on the drilled-fiber relation.  This run does not show that relation
is a formal consequence of the other nine; that requires a derivation, not an
enumeration.

Not established: anything about which sheet, if any, is pi_1 of a geometric
manifold.  The identification of a presentation with pi_1(V_aud) is the
paper's reconstruction step and is untouched here.  Lidman and Piccirillo's
contrary computation stands unreconciled, exactly as recorded in Run 67.
