Certified based monodromy
-------------------------

`paper_bridge.py` uses explicit rail parallels with literal one-edge whiskers
from p.  It verifies phi0(x,y)=(r,s) as vertex paths, the surface relator, and
the drilled-e free basis <x,y,r>.  Transporting the complete whiskered paths
through the open beta stack gives

  x -> y^-1,  y -> yx,  r -> r,  s -> s.

The open-stack calculation replays 17,839 elementary Tietze eliminations; all
remaining residuals Dehn-reduce to the identity in the genus-2 surface group.
Thus the reported unbased-vs-based monodromy failure is not reproduced for the
current model and these explicit whiskers.
