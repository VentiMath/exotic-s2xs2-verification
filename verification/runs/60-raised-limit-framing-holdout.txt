Run 60: a fourteenth framing holdout certified at a raised equation limit
============================================================================

Selected case
-------------

  n1_y2_ap1_bm1_jap1_jbm1
  half drift: n1_y2
  surgery signs: (+1,-1)
  counterfactual framing shift: (j_a,j_b)=(+1,-1)

This was one of the five cases left inconclusive by Run 59 at KBMAG's
default 32,767-equation ceiling in both raw relator orders. The exact same
fill-then-base 97-relator presentation was retried with

  maxeqns = 300000
  tidyint = 100
  ordering = shortlex

No relator, generator, or alphabet-order change was made.

Result
------

The raised-limit completion returned successfully, the resulting rewrite
system is confluent, and each of the four original group generators reduces
to the identity. The proof-producing rerun generated an ancestry history,
which compiled without fallback searches to a compact certificate containing
2,417 derivation-DAG records.

Independent replay:

  Python: VERIFIED TRIVIAL 2417 proof records
  Ruby:   ALL RUBY FILLED-GROUP CHECKS PASSED: 2417 DAG records

Both verifiers start from the exact 97-relator presentation bound into the
certificate by SHA-256. They independently check every free-group identity,
every derivation edge, and the four identity roots.

Boundary and tally
------------------

Before switching to this case, the other n1_y2 diagonal holdout
n1_y2_ap1_bp1_jap1_jbp1 was tried unchanged at the same 300,000-equation
ceiling. That attempt was stopped without a verdict; the interruption
proves neither triviality nor nontriviality and should not be
read as evidence that this case is harder than the one certified here.

Run 60 changes the repository's current certified robustness tally from 95/5
to 96/4: 96 trivial, 4 inconclusive, and no nontriviality witness. These are
auxiliary counterfactual framing shifts and do not gate the paper's j=0 proof.
