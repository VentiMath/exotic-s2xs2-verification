Capability finding
------------------

The live tree contains 1,344 Acorn source files and substantial infrastructure
for groups, normal subgroups, equivalence-class quotient representatives,
modules, finite vectors and matrices, topology, and graphs. The current project
roadmaps still list free groups/presentation-style quotients as unfinished.
No existing module supplies finite group presentations, word rewriting, or a
derivation-certificate checker.

Acorn implementation decision
-----------------------------

The shortest high-assurance route is a semantic group-presentation calculus:
interpret signed words in every target Group, prove each certificate rule
preserves equality, and derive that every generator is the identity under any
assignment satisfying the relators. This proves the needed triviality statement
without first constructing a free group or quotient group.

The DeepSeek handoff is exclusively for new `.ac` library modules: group words,
semantic presentations, derivation objects and soundness, shared finite
derivation sequences, presentation triviality, free and presented groups,
rank-two bilinear forms, and later chain/simplicial infrastructure. External
certificate generators, parsers, GAP/KBMAG instrumentation, and audit-repository
changes are explicitly outside that handoff.
