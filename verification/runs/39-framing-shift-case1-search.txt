Case
----

  n0_y1_ap1_bp1_jap1_jbp0

This is the auxiliary j_alpha=+1 framing-discrepancy filling at signs (+,+).
It is not one of the paper's j=0 fillings and is not needed for the existing
simple-connectivity certificates.  Its purpose is to measure what would
happen under a hypothetical one-meridian framing error.

Verdict

expanded target), but neither generator has been proved trivial and no
