Run 78: the Wuebben dictionary, relator by relator, in the paper (2026-09-02)
============================================================================

Question
--------

Run 71 located the paper's displayed relation sheet inside Wuebben's
ancillary GAP families -- literally and uniquely his fixed member
(m,n) = (0,0), identity generator dictionary -- but the paper still said
only that the comparison with his fixed member was open, and the
supplement's D10 row cited "independent relation-sheet extraction" without
naming the artifact or the answer.  A reader of v2.4.0 was owed the
translation: which (m,n), which dictionary, and the diff.  This run writes
it down, as a proposition in the main paper, as a named artifact in the
supplement, and as a relator-by-relator table in the repository, and says
in each place exactly what it does not settle.

verification/luttinger/wuebben_dictionary/print_dictionary.py (new): the
converse of compare_sheets.py.  It imports the reducer and both
transcriptions from compare_sheets.py (whose source-hash check runs first),
takes the located parameters, and for each of the four sign sheets writes
the twelve relators of the paper's sheet beside Wuebben's decide2.g
relators at those parameters, names the parameter that makes each pair
agree (e3 = +1 for A s A^-1 = N y; e4 = -1 for B y B^-1 = M^-1 y x;
e5 = -1 for B s B^-1 = (r^-1 M^-1 r) s; n = 0, m = 0, (eA,eB) = (eps_A,eps_B)
for the two fillings; nothing for the other seven), checks that the rows
reproduce ours(), his decide2.g system at (0,0), and his fixed_v_certify.g
system at the located point as multisets, and prints the unified diff of
the two sorted relator lists.  Output: dictionary.txt.  Twelve of twelve
relators agree letter for letter in all four sheets; the diff is empty.
The file also records his two shift words in this paper's letters,

    lambda_alpha(n) = A x . ((r x)^-1)^n
    lambda_beta(m)  = (r^-1 M r) B . (s r^-1 s^-1)^m

and notes that the outside reader's beta shift r^m is a different word
away from m = 0.  SHA256SUMS now lists seven files.

What this establishes
---------------------

The translation the paper owed is in the paper.  At the level of finite
presentations there is nothing between this paper and Wuebben's fixed
member: same eight generators, same twelve relators, no offset, unique
among every system his own scripts define.  A reader who wants to know
"which member is V_aud" has the answer in one proposition and can replay
it in a second with no dependencies.

What this does not establish
----------------------------

Nothing about manifolds, as Run 71 already said.  Whether Wuebben's marked
bundle, tori, basings, and framings are those of Definition 2.1 is
D1--D9 and D11--D14, unchanged.  Whether Lidman--Piccirillo's drawn
manifold is this member is open and essential.  A shared sheet is a shared
reconstruction risk.
