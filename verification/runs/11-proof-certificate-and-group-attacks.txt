Proof-producing Tietze reduction
--------------------------------

The target complement starts with 99,863 generators and 321,702 relators.
fast_tietze.py now records every elementary elimination as

  [source relator id, eliminated generator, replacement word].

An independent replay does not repeat the priority-queue search.  At each
step it reconstructs the current source relator, asserts that the generator
occurs exactly once, recomputes the replacement from that relator, substitutes
it everywhere, and finally checks a SHA-256 digest of the resulting relators
and tracked words.  The target certificate contains 99,860 verified
eliminations and reduces to 3 generators / 78 relators.  Compressed size:
743 KB.  The T^4 regression also verifies 861 eliminations and deliberately
rejects a corrupted first step.

Geometric paper-generator bridge
--------------------------------

r_run.py now exports explicit edge-loop candidates for x,y,r,s,A,B:
five-chain cores a,b,e,d in the unobstructed (A0,J2) fiber slice and alpha/beta
base loops through the fixed fiber vertex p.  These are explicitly labelled
candidates because the paper's generator choices include particular whiskers.
A sign scan proves one peripheral identity by the elementary substitutions:

  coord_alpha_fiber_r-1_x-1_out+1 = identity.

No tested alpha-base, beta-fiber, or beta-reference candidate becomes the
empty word under elementary elimination.  This is inconclusive as a word
problem, but correctly prevents an unsupported identification with the
paper's whiskered generators.

Bounded attacks
---------------

* KBMAG KnuthBendix, all eight: nonconfluent; each generator normal form is
  unchanged.  No triviality certificate.
* KBMAG AutomaticStructure, shortest case 7: maximum word-difference bound
  exceeded.  Inconclusive.
* ACE hard strategy, case 7, each cyclic generator subgroup and the trivial
  subgroup: every run exhausted its table (index 0, 999,998 total cosets).
* Case 7 epimorphisms onto A5, PSL(2,7), A6, PSL(2,8): zero for each target.
* LowIndexSubgroupsFpGroup(case 7,10) did not finish during the bounded run and
  was interrupted; historical runs had already found no proper subgroup of
  index <=10.
* Proof-oriented local search uses only Nielsen automorphisms, multiplication
  of one relator by a cyclic conjugate of another, and certified
  occurrence-one elimination.  A 100,000-step deterministic-seed run shortened
  cases 0,3,5,7 to total lengths 189,260,231,177 respectively, but eliminated
  no generator.  The other cases stayed at GAP's lengths.

Conclusion
----------

The reduction from the triangulation is now replayable rather than merely
trusted, and all downstream presentations are stable artifacts.  None of the
bounded attacks proves triviality or nontriviality.  The best next proof route
is still geometric: certify the paper's specifically whiskered x,y,r,s,A,B
loops and Table 1 annulus boundaries as explicit triangulated 2-chains/van
Kampen diagrams.  Blindly enlarging coset tables has substantially lower
expected value.
