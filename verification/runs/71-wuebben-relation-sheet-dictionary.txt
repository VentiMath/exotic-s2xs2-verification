Run 71: locating the displayed relation sheet in Wuebben's ancillary families (2026-09-02)
=========================================================================================

Question
--------

Which member (m,n) of Wuebben's surgery family, and which point of his
relation-sign robustness family, has the same relation sheet as the one
displayed in this paper's "Relation sheet and product fillings" subsection?
Supplement clause D10 asserts (m,n)=(0,0) by "independent relation-sheet
extraction and exact comparison" against his text.  This run makes the
comparison against his *ancillary GAP source* instead, exhaustively, and
records the answer as a repository artifact.

Frozen input
------------

Wuebben's side is the arXiv:2608.17267v1 ancillary package, pinned:

    2cdd848816994561be29541322a6c8e197366cdf1ff1825646886d97734b78b2  e-print tar.gz
    e750ebb79ed8856388a1b598b7cd003cb096d47531066411ffe45acc2e2900b5  anc/scripts/decide2.g
    da77de354824d023c2f1939bbe72ad3cb2466224e813c1e6e2f466f230612e37  anc/scripts/fixed_v_certify.g

Two of his scripts define parametrized relation systems on the free group
<x,y,r,s,A,B,M,N>:

  decide2.g          (m,n) in {-1,0,1}^2 times (e3,e4,e5,eA,eB) in {+-1}^5   = 288 systems
                     lambda_alpha(n) = A x . ((r x)^-1)^n
                     lambda_beta(m)  = (r^-1 M^{-e5} r) B . (s r^-1 s^-1)^m
  fixed_v_certify.g  (m,n)=(0,0); every local choice varied: three correction
                     placements, two c-arc conjugators, meridian sign and
                     placement for the beta push-off, five relation signs;
                     for two alpha sections (y1/Ax and y2/Ar^-1)              = 8192 systems

Our side is the displayed sheet: surface relation, eight transport relations,
drilled-fiber relation, lambda_alpha = A x, lambda_beta = (r^-1 M r) B, and
the four sign sheets (eps_A, eps_B).

The two Wuebben scripts are vendored verbatim in
`luttinger/wuebben_dictionary/wuebben_anc/` (MIT; see NOTICE there).
`compare_sheets.py` verifies their SHA-256 digests before reading anything
and aborts on mismatch.  It is standard-library Python only: free-group
words are reduced by a self-contained twenty-line reducer that shares no
code with GAP, with the repository's pi1 module, or with any package.  It
tests every one of the 8,480 Wuebben systems for equality with each of our
four sheets as a multiset of freely reduced relators, then ASSERTS the
expected answer -- exactly one literal match per sheet per family at the
recorded parameters and no other match of any kind -- exiting nonzero
otherwise.

The one step the script cannot check about itself is the hand transcription
of the GAP definitions into Python.  The vendored sources sit beside it for
line-by-line inspection; an outside reader independently checked the
transcription on 2026-09-02.

Result
------

Each of our four sheets matches exactly ONE system in each family, and the
match is literal (identical reduced words), not merely up to inversion:

    decide2.g:          (m,n) = (0,0),  e3=+1, e4=-1, e5=-1,  (eA,eB) = (eps_A, eps_B)
    fixed_v_certify.g:  section y1/Ax; all three corrections left-placed;
                        deltaBs = deltaF = r^-1; eta = +1; beta correction pre-B;
                        e3=+1, e4=-1, e5=-1; (eA,eB) = (eps_A, eps_B)

Generator dictionary: the identity on (x, y, r, s, A, B, M, N).  Same
commutator convention [u,v] = u v u^-1 v^-1.  Same drilled-fiber relation
(his R3).  No (m,n) offset.

What this establishes
---------------------

At the level of finite presentations, V_aud's displayed sheet IS Wuebben's
fixed member V'_{0,0} on his y1/Ax section, at one specific point of his
robustness family.  Clause D10 is confirmed against his source, not just his
text, and the answer is unique across 8,480 candidates.  Consequently:

  * Run 70's four-sheet enumeration is contained in Wuebben's own
    `fixed_v_certify` run (which reports 4096/4096 trivial); the two GAP
    installations agree on those four rows.
  * The question "is Lidman-Piccirillo's V the same member as V_aud?" is the
    same question as "is it the same member as Wuebben's V'_{0,0}?".  There
    is no offset between this paper and Wuebben's in which a discrepancy
    could hide.  Whatever LP's parametrization is relative to his, it is
    that relative to ours.  That question is NARROWED, not closed: whether
    LP's drawn manifold is this member remains open and essential.
  * Wuebben's own decide2 log shows every system at n = -1 overflowing at
    400,000 cosets for all m, while n in {0,1} is uniformly trivial.  His
    beta-shift word is (s r^-1 s^-1)^m, which is not the r^m used in the
    2026-09-01 outside grid; any in-repo sensitivity grid should use his
    words to be comparable.

What this does not establish
----------------------------

Nothing about manifolds.  Equality of relation sheets is equality of two
strings that two authors each claim to have read off geometry.  The smooth
identification V_aud ~= V'_{0,0} remains exactly where the paper leaves it:
clauses D12-D14 of the supplement, open.  And a shared sheet means a shared
reconstruction risk: if one of the three conjugating-path lines is wrong,
it is wrong in both papers at once.  LP's contrary computation stands
unreconciled, as in Runs 67 and 70.

Replay
------

    cd luttinger/wuebben_dictionary && python3 compare_sheets.py

Standard library only.  Exit 0 means the sources hashed correctly AND the
asserted unique matches held; anything else is a failure.
