Run 66: sealed raw-complex-to-reduced-presentation Tietze transport (2026-08-29)
================================================================================

Question
--------

The proof-producing Tietze reduction from the raw simplicial complement
presentation (99,863 generators, 321,702 relators) to the small presentation
on which the filled-group derivation certificates are built was recorded as
`luttinger/r_tietze_certificate.json.gz`, but its input was never
serialized: `r_run.py` rebuilds the raw presentation and immediately
generates and self-checks a fresh certificate.  This run asked whether the
committed 99,859-step trace can be replayed from a sealed input by a
standalone checker that imports no geometry or simplification code.

Finding: the committed trace's input cannot be regenerated
----------------------------------------------------------

The committed `r_presentations.json` (4 generators, 95 relators) and
`r_tietze_certificate.json.gz` (99,859 steps; input digest `3fe3de86...`,
output digest `3b86f486...`) were written by the `python3 r_run.py`
invocation recorded in `runs/20` (2026-08-23) and committed to the working
repository on 2026-08-24 (commit `661e810`).  Runs 12, 15, and 20 invoke
`r_run.py` without fixing `PYTHONHASHSEED`.  Runs 10 and 11 used
`PYTHONHASHSEED=0` and report 99,860 eliminations reducing to 3 generators
and 78 relators.

The engine's raw presentation depends on the interpreter's string hash
seed.  `complex.py` stores every simplex as a `frozenset` of vertex tuples
that contain string labels (`('t', 0)`, `('c', 0, i)`, ...) inside Python
`set`s, and `pi1.py` (`Presentation.__init__`) builds the breadth-first
spanning tree, numbers the non-tree edges, and orders the triangle relators
by iterating those sets.  A different seed therefore gives a different
spanning tree, a different generator numbering, and a different relator
order — a different presentation of the same group, not a relabelling.

differ from the working repository only by removed unused imports):

So the pipeline is deterministic for a fixed seed and interpreter-version
independent, but the seed of the run that produced the committed
four-generator artifacts was not recorded, and the 32-bit seed space cannot
be searched at two minutes per trial.  The input bound by the committed
certificate is unrecoverable.  That certificate was verified only inside
its own generating process (`r_run.py` replays the certificate it has just
produced before writing it) and has never been replayed independently.  The
earlier STATUS statement that the committed trace "is checked while
`r_run.py` reconstructs its large input" was wrong: `r_run.py` never loads
the committed certificate; it overwrites it.  The two step counts in the
documentation were both real — 99,860 from the seeded runs 10–11, 99,859
from the unseeded run 20 — and are now explained rather than reconciled.

What is unaffected
------------------

The eight committed filled-group derivation certificates
(`proof_certificates/*.json.gz`) bind the committed four-generator
presentation by digest and prove its eight filled presentations trivial;
nothing in this run touches them, their manifest, or the downstream chain.
The path-level peripheral identification (`runs/30`) compares literal
simplicial loops and does not depend on any spanning-tree numbering.  What
is not independently certified is precisely the transport from the raw
simplicial presentation and its geometric loops to the committed
four-generator presentation and its tracked words.

The sealed transport
--------------------

seeded run — `PYTHONHASHSEED=0 python3 r_run.py`, the invocation documented
in runs 10 and 11 — with the raw input serialized before simplification:

Two standalone verifiers, `luttinger/verify_tietze_transport.py` and
`luttinger/verify_tietze_transport.rb`, load exactly these three files and
import nothing from the geometry, the simplifier, or each other (Python:
`argparse`, `gzip`, `hashlib`, `json`, `pathlib`; Ruby: `json`, `zlib`,
`digest`, `optparse`).  Each checks the input format and generator count,
recomputes the input digest, replays every recorded step `[i, g, rep]` —
accepting it only if generator `g` occurs exactly once in the current
relator `i` and `rep` is the replacement that relator itself implies (the
inverse of the prefix followed by the inverse of the suffix, inverted when
the occurrence is negative) — applies the substitution to every relator
containing `g` (cyclically reduced, empties dropped) and to every tracked
word (freely reduced), drops duplicate and inverse-duplicate relators,
recomputes the surviving generator set and the output digest, and finally
renumbers and compares the result with the committed presentation: the
renumbering map, the generator count, every relator in order, and every
tracked word by name.

Python 45 s, Ruby 38 s on an Apple M-series laptop.  The negative controls
negate the first letter of the first input relator, delete the first
recorded step, append a letter to the first recorded replacement, reverse
the first committed relator, and reverse the longest committed tracked word.

Fillings of the sealed presentation
-----------------------------------

The eight `paper_fillings` of the sealed 3-generator presentation were
certified with the run-29 pipeline unchanged: `export_kbmag_proof_inputs.py`
(GAP, `../bin/gap`) wrote the rewriting systems, the patched image
`luttinger-kbmag-proof` (`kbmag-proof/Dockerfile`) ran `kbprog -ve`,
`compile_kbmag_certificate.py` pruned each history, and both checkers
verified the result.  Six cases complete confluently with the export's
default filling-first relator order and kbprog defaults; the two default-
order holdouts, cases 0 (`n0_y1_p1_p1`) and 6 (`n1_y2_m1_p1`), halt at the
default equation limit and complete with `-me 300000 -t 100` (the raised
limit of runs 60–62), case 0 in filling-first order.  The exact rewriting
systems are in `sealed_transport/raw_proof_inputs/`, the per-case options in
`sealed_transport/kbprog_options.json`; the certificates verify against
`sealed_transport/r_presentations.json` independently of either.

Consequences
------------

`make_proof_manifest.py` now hashes both chains (46 files) and checks the
certificate inventory of each; `downstream_chain.py` binds the sealed
presentation and this transcript as evidence for `K_pi1_V_trivial`;
`proof_ledger.py` makes `C_pi1_V_trivial` depend on the sealed chain
(`M_sealed_tietze_transport`, `M_sealed_filled_group_derivations`) and
records the unseeded four-generator transport as the ledger's single
`software_trust` node, on which no claim depends
(`C_pi1_V_trivial_unseeded_chain` is the retained corroborating derivation).

Not re-derived here: the 100-case framing scan (runs 58–63) and the
based-monodromy Tietze chains (runs 12, 23, 32) are built on the
four-generator export or replayed only in-process by the generator's own
verifier; they are documented as such.
