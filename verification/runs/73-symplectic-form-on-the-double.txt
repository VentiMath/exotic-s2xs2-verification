Run 73: the symplectic form on the audit double, and the existence theorem (2026-09-02)
======================================================================================

Question
--------

Run 72 (#807) doubled the audit object along its own boundary involution
sigma_aud and settled the topological half: R_hat = R_aud u_sigma R_aud is a closed
genus-2 bundle over a closed genus-2 surface, Z_aud = V_aud u_sigma V_aud is R_hat
surgered on four disjoint interior tori missing a fiber F and the doubled section
Gamma_hat, with F.F = 0, Gamma_hat.Gamma_hat = 0, F.Gamma_hat = 1.  What was left
open was the symplectic half: a symplectic form on R_hat, positive on F and
Gamma_hat, for which all four tori are Lagrangian with Lagrangian-framed slopes.
Each copy has one (main.tex, lem:bundle-symplectic-form and thm:framing); do the
two agree under sigma_aud on a collar of the seam?  (Issue #804.)

Answer
------

Yes, with no new choices.  In the paper's flat product atlas the vertical form is
the single phi_0- and psi_0-invariant area form Omega on every fiber, and over the
seam collar the gluing sigma_aud is, fiber by fiber, the constant Omega-preserving
map phi_0; so Omega_vert descends to R_hat as it is.  On the base, with the
outward-normal-first convention the positive area form on a boundary collar is
ds ^ dt (t the boundary parameter, s outward-increasing), and the second copy's
collar coordinates are t' = 1 - t, s' = -s, so ds' ^ dt' = ds ^ dt: eta descends
too.  Hence

    omega_hat_K = Omega_hat_vert + K pi_hat^* eta_hat

is symplectic on R_hat for every K > 0, restricts to the paper's omega_K on each
copy, is positive on F (= Omega) and on Gamma_hat (= K eta_hat), and the four tori
sit in the interiors of the copies away from the seam collar, where
lem:bundle-symplectic-form and thm:framing apply verbatim.  Four Luttinger
surgeries (ADK Prop. 2.2) give a symplectic form on Z_aud with F and Gamma_hat
symplectic.

With that, the existence theorem is assembled from the audit object alone:

  * pi_1(Z_aud) = 1 by van Kampen (pi_1(V_aud) = 1, thm:audit-manifold);
    chi(Z_aud) = 2 chi(V_aud) - chi(M_h) = 4, so b_2 = 2; (F, Gamma_hat) has Gram
    matrix H, unimodular, hence a basis; even, so spin; smooth, so
    Kirby-Siebenmann vanishes; Freedman: Z_aud is homeomorphic to S^2 x S^2.
  * R_hat is aspherical (aspherical fiber and base), so minimal and neither
    rational nor ruled; Li: kappa(R_hat) >= 0; Ho-Li: Luttinger surgery
    preserves kappa, so kappa(Z_aud) >= 0; kappa(S^2 x S^2) = -infinity and
    kappa is a diffeomorphism invariant (Li): Z_aud is not diffeomorphic to
    S^2 x S^2.

Correction to Run 72's fragment
-------------------------------

Writing lem:double-form(i) exposed a wrong parenthetical in the proof of
cor:audit-double(a) in paper/sigma_aud.tex: it said that, viewed from the
orientation of Sigma_hat, the second copy's fiber and base orientations are
"both reversed; their product is unchanged".  Neither is reversed: the base
gluing rho is a reflection of the boundary circle, so Sigma_hat restricts to the
given orientation on both copies of Sigma_{1,1}, and the fiber gluing phi_0 is
orientation-preserving on F.  The corollary's statement and conclusion were
unaffected (R_hat is oriented compatibly with both copies either way); the
parenthetical is replaced by the factorwise statement.  lem:double-form(i)
depends on the corrected version.
