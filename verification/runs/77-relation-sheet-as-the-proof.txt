Run 77: the relation sheet as the proof of simple connectivity (2026-09-02)
===========================================================================

Question
--------

An outside reader of v2.4.0 proposed inverting Section 5: put the ten
relations of the relation sheet first, prove pi_1(V_aud) = 1 from them by
coset enumeration, and make "those ten relations hold" the geometric content
of the paper, with the sealed certificates as confirmation.  Four specific
points came with it: (1) the drilled-fiber relation is a formal consequence
of the other relations, so Lemma "the drilled-fiber relation" must not call
it a construction-specific fact; (2) the abelianization proposition and the
100-case scan are blind at unit slopes; (3) a proposed replacement control --
fiber-direction shifts of the filling words under which "coset enumeration
does not terminate at three million cosets"; (4) one sentence and, where it
matters, one picture per relation.  Issue #800's witness search (stage 2)
was to run first.

What was checked before changing anything
-----------------------------------------

GAP 4.16.1, native, ~/opt/gap-4.16.1 (scratch scripts; the permanent
artifacts are listed below).

  * Ten relations + (+,+) fillings: |G| = 1 in 0.25 s.  Without the
    drilled-fiber relation: |G| = 1 in 1.1 s.  All four sign sheets: 1.
    (Run 70's containerised artifact already recorded this.)
  * The drilled-fiber relation is a consequence of the four B-transport
    relations and the surface relation: substituting the B-action letter by
    letter into s^-1 r^-1 y x gives s^-1 r^-1 y x y^-1 (the two meridian
    factors M r r^-1 M^-1 cancel), and s^-1 r^-1 y x y^-1 (r^-1 s^-1 x)^-1
    is the conjugate s^-1 r^-1 [s,r]^-1 . ([x,y][r,s])^-1 . [s,r] r s of
    the inverse surface relator.  Both are free-group identities.  The
    reader is right; the v2.4.0 prose of Lemma 5.8 was wrong.
  * Generation is NOT automatic (in F(a,m) the elements a^2 m a^-1 and m
    map onto F/<<m>> = <a> yet generate a proper subgroup: fold the
    Stallings graph).  Coset enumeration of the sealed 78-relator Q over the
    subgroup generated by the eight tracked words geom_x .. geom_N: index 1
    (28 ms); over the six without the meridians: index 1.
  * H_1 = 0 for all nine fiber-direction shifts (n, m) in {-1,0,1}^2 of the
    (+,+) fillings and for all four sign sheets.  The reader's point (2) is
    right.
  * The reader's control (3) does not hold up.  Every shifted unit-slope
    filling enumerated to completion is trivial:
        (+,+), shift (n,m) = (-1,-1): |G| = 1, 123 s, after GAP raised the
            table limit past 4,000,000 cosets;
        (-1,0): 89 s;  (-1,+1): 588 s;  the other six of the nine: < 1 s;
        the reader's literal words, lambda_alpha . c^-1 and
            lambda_beta . r^-1: |G| = 1, 28 s;  alpha shift alone: 92 s.
    "Does not terminate at three million cosets" is a table-size artifact:
    a fixed ceiling of 4,000,000 or 16,000,000 cosets overflows in seconds
    on the same groups that GAP's retry ladder then finishes.
    Non-termination is not evidence of nontriviality, and a referee who
    reruns it with a larger table watches the control collapse.
  * Witness search (#800), the "ours" family: the twelve cases are the
    paper's sheet with the alpha filling shifted by one fiber direction
    (n = -1), the beta filling shifted by m in {-1,0,1}, and all four
    (eps_A, eps_B).  Stage 1b (ceiling 16,000,000): every case overflows in
    15 s.  Stage 1c (GAP's Size ladder to 32,000,000): cases 1--4 and 6
    trivial (30--170 s); case 5 trivial (89 s, scratch run); case 9 trivial
    (588 s, scratch run); the remaining cases are recorded in
    witness_search/logs/ as they finish.  Stage 2 on case 9 (GQuotients
    onto every nonabelian simple group of order below 10^5, low-index
    subgroups to index 7): no quotient, no proper subgroup -- as it must be,
    the group being trivial.  No unit-slope filling of the sheet that is
    provably not simply connected has been found; the only provable
    negative control remains the non-unit slopes of the abelianization
    proposition.

What was done
-------------

paper/main.tex:

  * New Section 5.1 "The relation sheet and the collapse", before the
    normal-frontier subsection.  It fixes the eight based loops (fiber rail
    parallels x, y, r, s in the fiber over a base point off the spine; base
    lifts A, B through the p-section, each disjoint from its own spine loop
    and crossing the other once; whiskered meridians M, N), states the ten
    relations as Lemma 5.1, and proves them by the membrane mechanism: the
    membrane of a fiber loop along A meets T_beta exactly at the loop's
    intersections with e, the membrane along B meets T_alpha exactly at its
    intersections with c, each intersection inserting the whiskered meridian
    with the local sign.  Since y meets c once (c_y), s meets c once (c_s =
    phi_0(c_y)) and e once (s_e), and x, r meet neither, five transport
    relations are the bare monodromy actions and three carry one meridian
    each: AsA^-1 = N y (T_beta at s_e, whisker s_2 = N's own), ByB^-1 =
    M^-1 y x (T_alpha at c_y, whisker y_1 = M's own), BsB^-1 =
    (r^-1 M^-1 r) s (T_alpha at c_s, the initial segment of s, the same
    crossing and whisker that give lambda_beta its conjugated meridian).
    The drilled-fiber derivation is written out.  Signs and letter placement
    are attributed to the sealed transport certificates, not inferred.

What this establishes
---------------------

A referee can now verify simple connectivity from one page: the ten
relations and two filling words (Lemma 5.1, Corollary 5.4), the generation
statement (Lemma 5.2, one enumeration), and the collapse (Theorem 5.3, one
enumeration), with the geometric burden located exactly: that the relations
hold, and that the two longitude words are the product-framed longitudes.
The certificate apparatus is confirmation and derivation-from-triangulation,
no longer the only proof.

What this does not establish
----------------------------

  * The whisker-level facts -- which crossing, which whisker, which sign --
    are still supplied by the sealed transport certificates and the
    peripheral lemmas.  A simplicial filling that reads pi_1(V_aud) from a
    triangulation of V_aud itself, with no whisker, is the next machine
    milestone and is not done.
  * No provably non-simply-connected unit-slope filling of the sheet is
    known.  The reader's proposed control is a timing artifact.
