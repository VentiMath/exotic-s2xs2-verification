This is the auxiliary j_alpha=+2 framing-discrepancy filling at signs (+,+).
It is not one of the paper's j=0 surgery groups and its undecided status does
not weaken the paper verification.

Previously recorded bounded searches
------------------------------------

The group has trivial abelianization.  ACE remained open after 12,499,998
cosets at the 50,000,000-word workspace limit.  No epimorphism was found onto
A5, PSL(2,7), A6, PSL(2,8), PSL(2,11), or PSL(2,13), and the only subgroup of
index at most 8 found was the whole group.  These are bounded non-witness
results and do not prove triviality.

reversing the two raw lists gives lengths 913 and 907.  Thus 876 is the best
of the four tested deterministic orderings.

Proof-preserving reductions
---------------------------

1. Seeded Nielsen basis search reduces 876 to 609 letters.
2. Replayable relator multiplication reduces this to 602 letters, retaining
   2 generators and 9 relators.  Eleven of twelve seeds reached 602; one
   remained at 609.
3. Naming the automatically selected highest-saving repeated cyclic word and
   searching in the resulting three-generator presentation produced a new
   2-generator presentation with only 8 relators and total length 532 in two
   independent seeds.
4. Restarting from 8/532 and repeating the named-block cycle reaches a
   439-letter temporary-generator presentation.  All eight tested elimination
   routes return to the same 8/532 presentation, so this is the present
   one-block fixed point.

Every retained row was replayed before being written: generator Nielsen
automorphisms, named-block expansion, relator multiplications, and elementary
Tietze elimination certificates all matched their stored outputs.

Verdict and next attack
-----------------------

INCONCLUSIVE.  No nontrivial witness and no triviality certificate is known.
The strongest current proof target is the 439-letter temporary-generator
presentation, or the 8-relator/532-letter two-generator presentation.  The
next useful proof-producing escalation is a second named generator (or
relator-basis annealing while the first temporary generator remains present),
not a larger ACE workspace.
