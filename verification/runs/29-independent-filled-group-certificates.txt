Independent verification
------------------------

From `luttinger/`:

  python3 verify_kbmag_certificate.py proof_certificates/*.json.gz
  python3 make_proof_manifest.py --check

Observed output for every case:

  VERIFIED TRIVIAL

The checker does not import the compiler and does not invoke GAP or KBMAG. It
binds each certificate to the exact `r_presentations.json` SHA-256 digest and
checks, in topological order:

  * every formal-inverse cancellation axiom;
  * every initial equation against a cyclic conjugate or inverse of an input
    relator;
  * every critical overlap from its two already-proved parent equations;
  * every literal rewrite step, including rule number and word position;
  * every equation change made during completion tidying; and
  * one proved `[letter] = []` rule for each of the eight monoid letters.

Only equality in the presented group is used: free cancellation, insertion of
an already-proved equation in a word context, inversion, and cyclic conjugacy.
No confluence claim is needed once every generator is explicitly proved equal
to the identity.

Certificate generation
----------------------

The generator is deliberately outside the trust boundary. For reproducibility:

  python3 export_kbmag_proof_inputs.py
  docker build -t luttinger-kbmag-proof:local kbmag-proof
  python3 kbmag_history.py raw_proof_inputs/CASE.rws \
      raw_proof_history/CASE.proof.log
  python3 compile_kbmag_certificate.py \
      raw_proof_history/CASE.proof.log proof_certificates/CASE.json.gz \
      --case INDEX

Repeat the last two commands for the eight manifest cases, then run the
independent verification commands above. The proof-logging patch records data
that upstream `kbprog -ve` otherwise loses when it silently rewrites and
renumbers equations during tidying. Raw histories are reproducible scratch
artifacts and are not committed; the pruned certificates, original RWS inputs,
patch, checker, compiler, and hashes are committed.
