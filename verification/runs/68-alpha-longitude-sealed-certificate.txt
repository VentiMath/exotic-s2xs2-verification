Run 68: sealed complement certificate for the alpha longitude (2026-08-29)
============================================================================

Question
--------

Does the literal word `lb_a_y1` in the sealed 3-generator complement
presentation equal the paper-coordinate word `geom_A * geom_x`?  Run 15
reported this equality over a pre-export presentation, but that reduction did
not reproduce over either committed export.  The correct target in the sealed
presentation freely reduces to a nonempty word of length 72.

`luttinger/alpha_residual/build_input.py` reads only
`luttinger/sealed_transport/r_presentations.json` and writes

78-relator complement presentation, the three named word factors, and the

No filling relator is present.  The target and all 78 relators are copied into
the certificate and checked against the source by digest and literal equality.

Proof generation
----------------

The patched KBMAG `kbprog -ve` was run on the complement alone with
`-me 50000 -t 100`.  It stopped at the 50,000-equation limit after the final
tidy pass, leaving 49,990 active rules.  Full confluence is neither obtained
nor required.  The untrusted compiler parsed 67,838 historical rule records
and found a 61-step directed reduction of the target to the empty word.  It
retained the complete ancestry cone of those rules:

    2,506 proof records
    61 target rewrite steps
    0 compiler fallback searches

Conclusion
----------

The complement group itself satisfies

    lb_a_y1 = geom_A * geom_x.

Thus the mathematical conclusion of Run 15 is now established over the
sealed presentation, although Run 15's original evidence was not reproducible
and is explicitly superseded by this run.  No filled-group triviality result
is used.  The analogous 113-letter beta coordinate residual remains
uncertified; the direct beta boundary longitude used by the fillings is
unaffected.
