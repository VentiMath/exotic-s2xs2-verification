Run 72: the intrinsic boundary involution sigma_aud and the audit double (2026-09-02)
====================================================================================

Question
--------

The downstream chain (Run 64, downstream_chain_certificate.json) reaches Theorem A
-- an exotic S^2 x S^2 -- through exactly one assumption, A_source_formalization_D
(clauses D1-D14), and the only reason that assumption sits on the Theorem A path
is the external item E_lp_double: Lidman-Piccirillo's "sigma", a free
orientation-reversing bundle involution of S^3_0(Q), applied to V_aud by
transfer.  Can the same doubling structure be defined on the audit object itself,
from its own data, with no reference to whose manifold it is?  (Issue #807.)

Answer
------

Yes, and it is one line.  On the boundary mapping torus M_h of R_aud, with h the
commutator of the clutching maps phi_0 and psi_0, put

    sigma_aud [z, t] = [ phi_0(z), 1 - t ].

It respects the seam (z,1) ~ (h z, 0) iff phi_0 h phi_0^-1 = h^-1, and that holds
for any commutator of phi_0 with anything, in any order, because phi_0 is an
involution: phi_0 (phi_0 psi_0 phi_0^-1 psi_0^-1) phi_0^-1 = psi_0 phi_0 psi_0^-1
phi_0 = h^-1.  It reverses orientation (phi_0 preserves the fiber's, the
reflection reverses the base's), preserves the section boundary circles
(phi_0 fixes p and O), and is NOT free (it fixes [p,1/2], [O,1/2], [p,0], [O,0]).
Freeness is used by LP only for the quotient W = V/sigma (Theorem B); the
existence argument for Theorem A never uses it.

What the control checks
-----------------------

On the based monodromy actions Phi = (phi_0)_* : x->r, y->s, r->x, s->y and
Psi = (psi_0)_* : x->y^-1, y->yx, r->r, s->s -- the transport relations of the
displayed sheet with the meridians deleted; Phi(x)=r, Phi(y)=s are certified as
literal based paths by paper_bridge.py -- as automorphisms of the free group on
x,y,r,s, exactly, not up to inner automorphism:

  * Phi o Phi = id; Phi carries the surface relator to a conjugate; Psi fixes it.
  * Psi has the exact inverse x->xy, y->x^-1.
  * For all four orderings/orientations of the commutator h of Phi and Psi,
    Phi o h o Phi^-1 = h^-1 on every generator.
  * The same on H_1(F) = Z^4 as 4x4 matrices.
  * (h phi_0)^2 = id, so the seam fixed set of sigma_aud is the fixed set of an
    involution containing p and O.

Observation, not a claim: on H_1(F) the boundary monodromy is block diagonal,
[[1,-1],[1,0]] (+) [[0,1],[-1,1]] -- a trefoil monodromy and its inverse, i.e.
the square knot T(2,3) # T(2,-3), whose 0-surgery LP call S^3_0(Q).  Consistent
with the boundary being S^3_0(Q); nothing here identifies it.

What this establishes
---------------------

Every TOPOLOGICAL input the supplement's Theorem A propositions take from
E_lp_double is now a statement about the audit object with a written proof:
R_aud u_sigma R_aud is a closed genus-2 bundle over a closed genus-2 surface;
Z_aud is that bundle surgered on four disjoint interior tori missing F and
Gamma_hat; Gamma_hat is closed of genus 2 with square 0; F.F = 0, F.Gamma_hat = 1.

What this does not establish
----------------------------

The symplectic half.  A symplectic form on the closed bundle R_aud u_sigma R_aud,
positive on F and Gamma_hat, for which all four tori are Lagrangian with
Lagrangian-framed slopes, is not constructed here.  Each copy has one (the
paper's split-form lemma and framing theorem); what is missing is that the two
can be made to agree under sigma_aud on a collar of the seam -- the "descends and
standardizes both collars" item of the paper's own symplectic review list.  That
is issue #804.  Until it lands, Theorem A is not yet unconditional; after it,
#808 recuts the chain and #809 writes the theorem.

Also unchanged: LP's contrary computation stands unreconciled (Runs 67, 70, 71);
whether Z_aud is anyone else's manifold is the attribution track, not this one.
