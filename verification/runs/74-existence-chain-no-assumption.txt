Question
--------

The machine-checked dependency chain, luttinger/downstream_chain.py (Run 64),
still reached Theorem A through E_lp_double, E_lp_regluing and the one
assumption A_source_formalization_D (D1--D14), because it was written as a

existence theorem about Z_aud depends on nothing from Lidman--Piccirillo,
nothing from Wuebben, and no assumption -- and make the checkers refuse any
chain that does.  (Issue #808.)

What this establishes
---------------------

The machine-checked dependency structure of Theorem A' about Z_aud has no
assumption, no item from Lidman--Piccirillo, and no item from Wuebben, and
the checkers will reject any future edit that reintroduces one.  Its inputs
are this repository's certificates (pi_1(V_aud)=1, Gamma_hat^2=0, the framing
identification, sigma_aud), three written proofs in paper/ bound by hash, and
nine standard published theorems.

What this does not establish
----------------------------

  * Nothing mathematical is new here; Runs 72--73 carry the mathematics.
    This run changes what the chain SAYS it depends on, and makes that
    checkable.
  * One provenance contact with Wuebben remains inside the existence
    closure and is not a premise about his manifold: M_independent_paper_
    dictionary (Run 54) reads his paper's text to reconstruct the marked
    data that DEFINE the audit object, and G_topological_smooth_reroute
    cites it for that definition (its note's v2.0.0 clarification says so
    explicitly).  Theorem A' is about V_aud as defined; the definition's
    origin is not a hypothesis.  If a fully origin-free ledger is wanted,
    def:Vaudit can be made the sole source of the marked data and that node
    removed from the reroute's premise list -- a separate, documented edit.
  * The paper itself: the two fragments are still not \input by main.tex,
    and main.tex still frames the doubling as conditional (#809).  The
    certificate binds paper/main.tex, so #809 will require a refreeze --
    intended: the chain tracks the text it certifies.
  * Attribution (whether Z_aud is anyone else's manifold) is untouched and
    remains where it was: attribution/ and the D1--D14 checklist.
