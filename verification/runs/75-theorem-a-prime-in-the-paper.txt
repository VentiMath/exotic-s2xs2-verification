What this establishes
---------------------

The manuscript now claims Theorem A' as its primary result, proves it in
Section 7 from its own earlier theorems and five named published results,
and the machine chain that certifies the argument binds the manuscript text
it certifies.  No sentence in the manuscript conditions any theorem on
D1-D14 or on Lidman-Piccirillo.

----------------------------

    version are still v2.3.0's.  The release cut (reserve DOI, replace the

    step.

    reported contrary computation, stand exactly as before.
  * Theorems B and C of the source are not claimed for the audit object.
