Input bridge
------------
The triangulation-derived complement presentation now contains explicit,
commonly based paper-coordinate words x,y,r,s,A,B,M,N.  The preceding checks
certify the required filling directions:

  dir_base(T_alpha) = A x
  dir_base(T_beta)  = (r^-1 M r) B

The beta M exponent is +1 because the independently certified M3 convention
is BsB^-1=(r^-1 M^-1 r)s, so epsilon=-1 and Section 8.4 uses M^-epsilon.

Method
------
group_attack.py paper-kb-all adds, directly to the proof-reduced machine
presentation of pi_1(C),

  M (A x)^eA,
  N ((r^-1 M r) B)^eB

for all (eA,eB) in {+1,-1}^2.  The adjacent half-drift family replaces Ax by

  A r^-1 = A x (r x)^-1.

GAP first applies
IsomorphismSimplifiedFpGroup.  KBMAG then runs KnuthBendix on the resulting
presentation.  A case is called trivial only if every free generator reduces
to the identity; here every rewriting system also completed confluently.

Conclusion and scope
--------------------
This resolves the perfect-group/triviality blocker for the four geometric n=0
fillings. It is a direct computation in the triangulation-derived complement
group, not a comparison of low-index fingerprints and not an unfinished coset
enumeration. The four adjacent Ar^-1 relation systems are also certified
trivial. As the paper states, that second run is a robustness/re-indexing check
and is not needed for the specified n=0 manifold. Run 19 records the subsequent
geometric y2 alpha bridge and the still-inconclusive alternate beta-coordinate
comparison.

The older eight exported fillings used two arbitrary c[0]-based half-drifts
and presentation-tree basings.  Their exact simultaneous conjugacy to the
paper-coordinate meridian/longitude pairs should still be recorded before
calling those particular word presentations identical.  That bookkeeping is
no longer an obstruction to either paper half-drift family, which is filled
directly using explicit y_1- and s_2-based boundary loops.
