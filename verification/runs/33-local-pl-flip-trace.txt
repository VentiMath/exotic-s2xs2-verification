Run 33: local PL flip-trace certificate (2026-08-25)
=========================================================

Purpose
-------

Replace visual or expert inspection of the alternative twist cobordism by a
finite audit of its assembled tetrahedra. The checker is deliberately
downstream of construction: it does not call the builder's flip, trace, or
structural checking helpers.

What is checked
---------------

`luttinger/pl_flip_trace.py` rebuilds the alternative beta stack and then
reads only its final simplices. It assigns every tetrahedron to one of the 64
slabs and reconstructs all 128 cone vertices. For every cone it verifies:

* exactly two lower and two upper triangles on the same four labels;
* complementary old and new diagonals;
* exactly four side squares with the correct staircase triangulations;
* an 8-vertex, 18-edge, 12-triangle connected closed link of Euler
  characteristic 2, with every vertex link circular;
* a 12-tetrahedron cone star whose boundary is exactly that link; and
* vertex-disjoint quadrilaterals for simultaneous flips.

For each complete slab it additionally verifies that every tetrahedron
outside the cone stars is one of the three exact staircase prisms over an
untouched lower triangle. The slab boundary is exactly its lower and upper
marked fiber and its change of triangulation is exactly the union of the
logged flips. A mutation control deletes one cone tetrahedron and is rejected.

Global manifold audit
---------------------

  stack f-vector                 [5718,40456,69296,34560]
  interfaces                     64
  elementary flips               128
  complete boundary triangles    352
  boundary components            two marked copies of the genus-2 fiber

The checker audits all 5,718 vertex links of the open stack. Every interior
link is a connected closed combinatorial surface with Euler characteristic 2;
every boundary link is a connected combinatorial surface with one circular
boundary and Euler characteristic 1. Thus all links are respectively
2-spheres or 2-disks. In particular, the check is stronger than a global
pseudomanifold/f-vector test.

Conclusion and exact theorem boundary
-------------------------------------

Every implemented local move is the labeled cone-ball trace of the stated
2-2 bistellar flip, and the remainder of every slab is the literal product
trace. Together with Run 32's exact based-pi1 action, the code no longer asks
a reviewer to infer the mapping class from a picture or from homology.

The residual PL input has been reduced to the elementary general facts that a
connected closed combinatorial surface of Euler characteristic 2 is S^2, its
cone is a PL 3-ball, and the labeled 2-2 ball trace realizes the corresponding
bistellar move. These facts are exposed in the certificate rather than hidden
inside a construction claim.

Reproduce from the repository root:

  python3 luttinger/pl_flip_trace.py \
      --check \
      --output luttinger/pl_flip_trace_certificate.json
