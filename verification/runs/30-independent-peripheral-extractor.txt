Run 30: independent peripheral extractor (2026-08-25)
======================================================

Question
--------

Can the four geometric loops used in the direct fillings be recovered from
the marked triangulation without reusing the original complement/peripheral
implementation, and in particular without silently forgetting the paper's
based y_1 and s_2 whiskers?

Independent implementation boundary
-----------------------------------

`luttinger/independent_peripheral_extractor.py` takes only the marked
triangulation returned by `bundle.build_bundle`. It does not import
`complement.py`, `pi1.py`, `paper_bridge.py`, `peripheral_bridge.py`,
`r_run.py`, or `sweep.py`.

After the triangulation is built, the new program separately implements the
induced complement, first-derived frontier, orientations, dual meridians,
product-square push-offs, frontier retraction, marked loops, literal lassos,
and canonical serialization. No finite-presentation or word solver is used.
The comparison is at the level of literal simplicial paths, avoiding the
nondeterministic numbering of roughly 100,000 spanning-tree edges.

Results
-------

The independent route reproduces the global counts recorded by the original:

  K f-vector                  [9156,116714,356728,403152,153984]
  induced complement          8860 vertices, 108722 edges
  derived-frontier vertices   113336
  T_alpha f-vector            [24,72,48]
  T_beta f-vector             [272,816,544]

It independently finds the unique crossings

  c_y = ('b',-1,4),     s_e = ('d',-1,4),

and extracts:

  geom_M:     oriented 8-edge dual meridian, literal y_1 prefix;
  lb_a_y1:   28-edge frontier push-off, same y_1 prefix,
             A lift plus the negative c half;
  N_grid:      oriented 8-edge dual meridian, literal s_2 prefix;
  geom_N:      the paper/Table-1 transport A*N_grid^-1*A^-1;
  lb_b_s2:  136-edge frontier push-off, same s_2 prefix,
             B lift with zero e drift.

The local complement lassos have 18, 40, 24, and 154 edges respectively; the
transported paper N has 29 edges after immediate endpoint joins. The
independent alpha-s grid has one beta-torus hit and punctured boundary lengths
8 and 12. A separate local 66-step elementary Tietze calculation reduces both
the grid detour and the oriented dual meridian to the same generator of a
free rank-one local complement, while their inverse comparison has exponent
two. This recovers the orientation and the outer-boundary transport used in
the paper's M1 relation without importing the original sweep code.

All canonical hashes and complete source metadata are frozen in
`luttinger/independent_peripheral_certificate.json`.

Sensitivity controls distinguish the two alpha half-sections, the y_1 and
opposite-side y_2 whiskers, and an oriented meridian from its inverse. Thus
the certificate changes under the based/unbased, half-drift, and orientation
errors under audit.

Conclusion and scope
--------------------

No discrepancy was found. A second implementation recovers the same semantic
peripheral data used by the direct fillings, including the distinction between
the source-side local N_grid and the transported paper N:

  (geom_M, lb_a_y1),    (geom_N, lb_b_s2).

This independently checks the extraction, not the construction of the marked
triangulation: both routes still share `bundle.build_bundle`. It also does
not replace the standard smooth input identifying product/fibered and
Lagrangian framings; that remains paper Lemma 8.2 plus the chart audit.

As an additional necessary diagnostic, `group_attack.py word-all` now tests
the commutators of the two exported meridian/longitude pairs. The alpha
commutator has a positive KB identity reduction. The base complement system is
nonconfluent on the beta commutator, so that reduction is inconclusive; the
word nevertheless dies in the computed class-6 p-group quotients for
p=2,3,5 (orders 2^55, 3^55, and 5^55, respectively). The geometric
grid/frontier certificate above, rather than those
finite quotients, is the evidence for the beta basing.

Reproduce from the repository root:

  python3 luttinger/independent_peripheral_extractor.py \
      --check \
      --output luttinger/independent_peripheral_certificate.json

The check regenerates the certificate and demands byte-for-byte equality.
The auxiliary commutator diagnostic is reproduced with:

  cd luttinger
  python3 group_attack.py word-all --gap ../bin/gap
