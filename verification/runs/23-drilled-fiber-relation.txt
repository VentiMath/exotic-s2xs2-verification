============================================================================

Target
------

  kappa3 = s^-1 r^-1 y x,
  pi1(F - nu(c),p) = Free<x,r,kappa3>,

Method
------
`paper_bridge.py::certify_kappa3` removes the vertices of the chordless c
core and computes the fundamental group of the resulting induced subsurface.
It constructs geometric spanning-tree loops, finds a third loop which together
with the already certified x and r loops freely generates the rank-three
group, and searches in that geometric basis for the class
s^-1 r^-1 y x. The resulting deterministic based edge loop has length 31 and is disjoint
from c vertex by vertex.

The closed-surface equality is checked after proof-producing elementary
Tietze reduction and Dehn reduction against the unique genus-2 surface
relator. Since F - nu(c) is an incompressible subsurface, this identifies the
drilled-fiber class. A second free-group calculation proves that the actual
triple x,r,kappa3 is a basis, with explicit inverse-basis words.

Finally, the complete 31-edge path is transported through the open beta flip
stack. Its boundary residual against the literal top path r^-1 s^-1 x is
reduced using 17,839 elementary Tietze steps; the certificate is replayed and
the remaining surface word Dehn-reduces to the identity.
