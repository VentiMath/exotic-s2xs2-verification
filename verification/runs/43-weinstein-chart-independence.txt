Run 43: Weinstein-chart independence for Lemma 8.2 (2026-08-26)
================================================================

Two Weinstein charts for the same Lagrangian torus give isotopic sufficiently
small constant-momentum push-offs in the torus complement. Hence changing the
chart cannot add a meridian to the Lagrangian framing.

After correcting the base parameterization, the chart transition is a
symplectomorphism germ of `T^*T` fixing the zero section. Conjugating it by
fiber dilation gives a symplectic Alexander isotopy to the identity. Applying
that isotopy to a small constant-momentum curve proves the required push-off
independence directly. The proof is in
`notes/lemma82_chart_independence_2026-08-26.md`.

Command:

  python3 luttinger/weinstein_chart_independence.py

  PASS dilation path limits to the identity at t=0
  PASS induced normal-quotient map is the identity
