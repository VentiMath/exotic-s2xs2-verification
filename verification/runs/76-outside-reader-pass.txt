Item 1
------

What the sources say
--------------------

  * C.-I. Ho and T.-J. Li, Luttinger surgery and Kodaira dimension, Asian
    J. Math. 16 (2012), 299--318, read in arXiv:1108.0479v2.  Theorem 1.1:
    "The Luttinger surgery preserves the symplectic Kodaira dimension."
    The surgery is the one of their Section 2.1 (after Auroux--Donaldson--
    Katzarkov): a (1,k)-surgery with respect to a Lagrangian framing of a
    Lagrangian torus, with the induced form; all manifolds oriented.  The
    proof blows down -1 spheres disjoint from the torus (Welschinger) and
    compares K^2 and K.[omega] on the common complement.
  * T.-J. Li, The Kodaira dimension of symplectic 4-manifolds, Clay Math.
    Proc. 5 (2006), 249--261 (Floer homology, gauge theory, and low-
    dimensional topology; Ellwood, Ozsvath, Stipsicz, Szabo, eds.), read
    from the volume PDF published by the Clay Mathematics Institute.
    Definition 2.2: kappa of a minimal (M, omega) is -infinity iff
    K.[omega] < 0 or K.K < 0.  Theorem 2.4 (M closed oriented, omega
    compatible with the orientation, (M, omega) minimal): (2) "(M, omega)
    has Kodaira dimension -infinity if and only if it is rational or
    ruled"; the text says "Property (2) follows from [41]", which is
    A.-K. Liu, Some new applications of general wall crossing formula,
    Gompf's conjecture and its applications, Math. Res. Lett. 3 (1996),
    569--585.  Rational and ruled are defined there by the underlying
    smooth manifold.  Page 251: "the Kodaira dimension of (M, omega) only
    depends on the oriented diffeomorphism type of M, i.e. if omega' is
    another symplectic form on M compatible with the orientation of M,
    then kappa(M, omega) = kappa(M, omega')."
  * T.-J. Li, Symplectic 4-manifolds with Kodaira dimension zero, J.
    Differential Geom. 74 (2006), 321--352: the source the Clay survey
    summarizes; bibliographic data confirmed on Project Euclid (DOI
    10.4310/jdg/1175266207).  Its internal theorem numbering was not
    read, so the paper cites the Clay survey by number and the JDG paper
    by title.
  * Bibliographic data for Liu and for the Clay survey confirmed against
    zbMATH (zbMATH 980873 and 5082593).

So (a) is Liu's theorem, (b) is Ho--Li Theorem 1.1 as used, and (c) is
proved for forms compatible with one orientation, not for the unoriented
diffeomorphism type.  The machine chain's S6 already used the oriented
form of (c) with the reflection of one S^2 factor; the manuscript prose
did not.

What was done for item 1
------------------------

paper/main.tex:

  * Proof of Theorem 2.3 rewritten in four steps.  Step 1: R_hat is
    aspherical, hence minimal and neither rational nor ruled (each of those
    contains a homologically nonzero embedded sphere), so by Liu's theorem
    [Liu], [LiClay, Theorem 2.4(2)] kappa(R_hat, omega_hat_K) != -infinity.
    Step 2: the four Luttinger surgeries of Corollary 7.5 each preserve
    kappa [HoLi, Theorem 1.1].  Step 3: a diffeomorphism psi to S^2 x S^2
    is made orientation-preserving by a reflection of one factor; the
    pullback of a product form omega_{a,b} is compatible with the
    omega_hat-orientation and has kappa = -infinity by Definition 2.2
    (S^2 x S^2 is minimal since its form is even; K evaluates to -2 on
    each factor sphere, so K.[omega_{a,b}] = -2a - 2b < 0).  Step 4:
    two forms compatible with one orientation have equal kappa [Li],
    [LiClay, p. 251]; contradiction.
  * Section 7.3 list: the Ho--Li item names Theorem 1.1 and the surgery
    definition it covers; the former Li item is split into a Liu item
